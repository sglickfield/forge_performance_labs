Coaching Recommendations
 Context for basketball. Vertical and horizontal power, first-step speed, and single-leg stability are highly relevant to rebounding,
finishing, and defensive slides. Grip is secondary but useful for ball security.
 Protect and progress current strengths. 40 m Sprint, Vertical Jump, Broad Jump sit above the typical band for age/sex. Continue
quality speed and/or power work with adequate recovery so these qualities remain assets rather than being eroded by high-volume
general training.
 Position-relevant power. Emphasize horizontal force (broad-jump quality, resisted accelerations) and reactive strength. Include
change-of-direction and deceleration work once linear power is stable.
 Retest cadence. Repeat the full battery (or the completed subset) after an 8–12 week focused block. Track the mid-thigh pull
longitudinally even though it lacks a published typical range.
